00Features

Deep engineering,
surface simplicity.

A native bubble-card panel living in your menu bar — every display action one or two clicks away.

One-click main display

Click any display in the panel to make it the main one — the screen the menu bar and Dock live on. No digging through System Settings.

Arrange, mirror, auto-layout

Place an external screen left or right, toggle extend/mirror in one click, or auto-arrange everything side by side — mirror groups stay intact.

Layout presets & hotkeys

Save a whole configuration — main display, positions, mirroring — as a preset, then restore it with one click or a global hotkey bound to it.

HiDPI-safe resolution & refresh

Switch any screen's resolution and refresh rate (60 ↔ 120Hz) right in the panel. HiDPI scaling is tagged and preserved — no tiny icons on 4K screens.

To-scale layout preview

Displays are drawn to scale in a live mini-map. Click a block to jump straight to that screen's parameters; hovering a row highlights its block.

Native, bilingual, accent-aware

A pure menu-bar app with no Dock icon. The panel tracks your system accent color live, and the UI switches between English and 简体中文 in one click.
